  <behavior>
    - Given a studentId and jobId:
      - Skill Score (40%): Cosine similarity between `student.skillVector` and `job.jobVector`.
      - Experience Score (20%): LLM assessment of student's experience vs JD.
      - Project Score (20%): LLM assessment of student's projects vs JD.
      - Potential Score (20%): LLM assessment of growth and skill adjacency.
    - Result: Total Score (0-100) + breakdown + analysis text.
  </behavior>
